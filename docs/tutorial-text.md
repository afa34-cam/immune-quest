# Tutorial text (Stealth Mission)

Everything Spike says, in order. Edit the wording here (or mark changes) and send it back: each line has an ID (e.g. **T3-2**) so changes can be matched to the right step.

- **Mood** = Spike's face for that step (happy, excited, worried, wink, thinking, evil, ninja).
- **Waits for your move** = no Next button; Spike moves on when the player does what he asks. Below his text the game adds a green line saying exactly what to select (e.g. "👉 Select Vertical Spread, then the lung."), generated automatically.
- **Real biology** = the blue box under the text.

## Outside the mission

- **Select-screen button:** "New here? Play the tutorial" / "Spike the virion shows you how to play Stealth Mission"
- **End screen title:** "Tutorial Complete"; line under it: "Ready for a real mission?"; buttons "To Patient" and "Real Mission".
- **Leaving (✕ or Restart):** "Leave the tutorial?"

## Turn 1

### Turn 1 · before Virus Spread

**T1-1** · _Mood: ninja_  
Hi, I’m **Spike**! In this practice mission you play as me as we try to sneak past the immune system.

> **Real biology:** One virus particle is called a **virion**. The points on my surface are **spike proteins**. Real viruses such as flu and SARS-CoV-2 have them too and use them to get into cells.

**T1-2** · _Mood: happy_  
These are three organs of the body: the **liver**, **lung** and **intestine**. Every square is a cell.

> **Real biology:** A virus can’t copy itself. It has to get inside a living cell and use the cell’s machinery to make new virus particles.

**T1-3** · _Mood: thinking_  
Our mission: infect one cell in the **bottom row** of any organ. I lose if the immune system clears all my infected cells, or boxes me in so I can’t spread.

**T1-4** · _Mood: evil_  
For now I can only infect the lung. That’s my **tropism**: the tissues a virus can infect. Grey tissues are off-limits… for now!

> **Real biology:** Spike proteins work like keys. A virus can only get into cells that have the matching lock (a **receptor**) on their surface.

**T1-5** · _Mood: excited_  
This is me. **Replication speed** is how fast I copy myself: the higher it is, the more I spread each turn. **Mutation rate** is how often changes are made when I copy myself.

**T1-6** · _Mood: thinking_  
Every turn has five steps. The first three happen by themselves: **Virus Spread**, **Immune Response** and **Mutation**. Then it’s your turn to **Pick Cards** and **Play Cards**.

**T1-7** · _Mood: happy · Button: "Start"_  
Everything that happens is written in the **mission log**. Select it to open or close it. Ready? Watch me spread!

### Turn 1 · after Virus Spread

**T1-8** · _Mood: ninja_  
That’s **Virus Spread**: my infected cells infect healthy cells below and beside them. Spreading down is most likely, and down is where the bottom row is!

### Turn 1 · before Immune Response

**T1-9** · _Mood: worried_  
Uh-oh, the immune system has noticed me. The cards it plays appear in the **Immune System** banner.

### Turn 1 · after Immune Response

**T1-10** · _Mood: thinking_  
**NK Cell** cleared one of my infected cells.

> **Real biology:** **Natural Killer (NK) cells** are part of the body’s first-response team. They destroy cells that look stressed or infected.

**T1-11** · _Mood: worried_  
**Cytokines** shields healthy cells so I can’t infect them for a few turns. **Blue** shields 1 cell, **Orange** a cross of 5 and **Red** a block of 9.

> **Real biology:** **Cytokines** are messengers and chemical alarm signals. Infected cells release them to warn their neighbours and call in immune cells.

**T1-12** · _Mood: wink_  
Tip: select **ⓘ** on any card to read what it does and the real biology behind it.

### Turn 1 · after Mutation

**T1-13** · _Mood: thinking_  
**Mutation**: each time I copy myself, mistakes creep into my genes (blueprint for how to copy). Some help me, some don’t. No mutation this time.

> **Real biology:** Viruses with genes made of RNA, like influenza virus, make lots of copying mistakes, so they mutate quickly. Other viruses, particularly if they have genes made of DNA, have proofreading to reduce these mistakes.

Your turn! You’re offered 3 cards and keep 2. Let’s pick **Vertical Spread** and **Horizontal Spread**.

**T1-15** · _Mood: happy_  
The cards you keep go into your hand, down here.

**T1-16** · _Mood: ninja · Waits for your move_  
Play **Vertical Spread**. It infects a healthy cell right below one of my infected cells.

**T1-17** · _Mood: wink · Waits for your move_  
Nice! You don’t have to play every card. Keep Horizontal Spread for later.

## Turn 2

### Turn 2 · after Immune Response

**T2-1** · _Mood: worried_  
**Antibody** shields a whole row of cells, and it doesn’t wear off!

> **Real biology:** **Antibodies** are Y-shaped proteins made by B cells. They stick to a virus’s surface proteins and stop it getting into cells.

**T2-2** · _Mood: thinking_  
And an **Orange Cytokine**: a cross of 5 shielded cells.

### Turn 2 · after Mutation

**T2-3** · _Mood: excited_  
A lucky mutation! I gained **Tropism** for the liver, so now I can infect it too.

> **Real biology:** A small change in a spike protein (key) can let a virus fit a new receptor (lock), so it can infect new kinds of cells.

### Turn 2 · your turn (Pick Cards → End Turn)

**T2-4** · _Mood: happy · Waits for your move_  
Keep **Antibody Escape: Lung** and **Replication Boost**.

**T2-5** · _Mood: evil · Waits for your move_  
That antibody row is in my way! **Antibody Escape: Lung** removes every antibody in the lung.

> **Real biology:** By changing the shape of its surface proteins, a virus can stop antibodies from recognising it. Influenza virus does this so often that the flu vaccine is updated every year.

**T2-6** · _Mood: excited · Waits for your move_  
**Replication Boost** adds +1 to my replication speed for the next Virus Spread. Watch the speed go up.

> **Real biology:** Copying faster makes more virus, but also more copying mistakes (mutations).

**T2-7** · _Mood: happy · Waits for your move_  
Keep Horizontal Spread for later.

## Turn 3

### Turn 3 · after Virus Spread

**T3-1** · _Mood: excited_  
The boost helped me spread two rows further!

### Turn 3 · after Immune Response

**T3-2** · _Mood: worried_  
Ouch! A **T Cell** cleared a whole row of my infected cells.

> **Real biology:** Cells show bits of the proteins inside them on their surface, on molecules called **MHC class I**. **T cells** check these and destroy cells that show signs of a virus.

**T3-3** · _Mood: worried_  
**Antiviral** lowered my replication speed by 1. For good!

> **Real biology:** Antiviral medicines block a step a virus needs to copy itself, so the infection spreads more slowly or not at all.

### Turn 3 · after Mutation

**T3-4** · _Mood: excited_  
Another mutation: I gained a **Cytokine Neutralisation (Orange)** card. It went straight into my hand.

### Turn 3 · your turn (Pick Cards → End Turn)

**T3-5** · _Mood: happy · Waits for your move_  
Keep **T-Cell Evasion: Lung** and **Latency**.

**T3-6** · _Mood: ninja · Waits for your move_  
Let’s get rid of that orange shield. **Cytokine Neutralisation (Orange)** removes a whole orange block.

> **Real biology:** Some viruses make proteins that mop up cytokines, so the alarm signal never reaches the neighbouring cells.

**T3-7** · _Mood: thinking · Waits for your move_  
T cells could be back. **T-Cell Evasion: Lung** cancels the next T Cell the immune system plays in the lung.

> **Real biology:** Some viruses hide the MHC class I “windows” on infected cells, so T cells can’t see that the cell is infected.

**T3-8** · _Mood: happy · Waits for your move_  
Keep Latency for next turn.

**T4-4** · _Mood: evil · Waits for your move_

> **Real biology:** Some viruses, like herpes viruses, can hide quietly inside cells for months or even years and wake up later.

> **Real biology:** **Regulatory T cells (Tregs)** are the immune system’s brakes. They stop it overreacting. Some viruses press that brake to weaken the attack.
