About
Immune Quest is a free educational game platform designed to empower young people with trustworthy knowledge about infections, the immune system, vaccines, and treatments.

Version 0.25
© 2026 · Developed by Arwen Altenburg with Claude (Sonnet 4.6, 5, Opus 5.5) & Google Gemini.

The Infection Game (legacy)
Original game developed by Dr AJ te Velthuis (Princeton University) and Dr Arwen Altenburg (University of Cambridge) with SKYLE games. Artwork by Ramon Calvante. A massive thank you to Dr Ingrida Olendraite for her assistance and to the University of Cambridge School of Biological Sciences and the British Society for Immunology for their financial support.

Privacy
Share anonymous play stats
Helps us understand how the game is used. No personal data collected.
We use GoatCounter, a privacy-first analytics tool, to count which games are opened and how often. It uses no cookies and never stores IP addresses or any other personal data — every visit is completely anonymous. These numbers help us show evidence of public engagement when reporting to the University and our funders, which helps keep Immune Quest free.

Play full-screen
Add Immune Quest to your Home Screen to play it like an app: full-screen, without the browser bars, and one tap away.
  1. Open this page in Safari. These steps may differ slightly when using another browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
  2. Tap the Share button next to the address bar.
  3. Tap View More, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, keep it switched on. Then tap Add.
  5. Open Immune Quest from the new icon on your Home Screen.
Progress saved here in the browser doesn't carry over to the Home Screen app. It starts fresh there.
