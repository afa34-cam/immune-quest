Immune Quest — Cancer Game
Colorectal Cancer
Cancer starts when a single cell in your body begins dividing out of control. In colorectal cancer, that cell is in the lining of your large intestine. The body's defence system constantly patrols for these rogue cells — but sometimes a tumour grows faster than the defences can keep up.

If it escapes into the bloodstream, it can reach other organs like the liver. Your job is to stop it before that happens.
