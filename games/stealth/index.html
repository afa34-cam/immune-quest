About
Immune Quest is a free educational game platform designed to empower young people with trustworthy knowledge about infections, the immune system, vaccines, and treatments.

The Infection Game (virus)
Original game developed by Dr AJ te Velthuis (Princeton University) and Dr Arwen Altenburg (University of Cambridge) with SKYLE games. Artwork by Ramon Calvante. A massive thank you to Dr Ingrida Olendraite for her assistance and to the University of Cambridge School of Biological Sciences and the British Society for Immunology for their financial support.
